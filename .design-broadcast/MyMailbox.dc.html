How the password is held

Encrypted at rest with a key held outside the database
Decrypted only server-side, only while a send is running
Never returned to the browser, not even to you
Deleted outright when you disconnect

Worth being clear-eyed: this is a real mailbox password sitting in your database. That's the cost of mail genuinely coming from you rather than from a shared account.
