  carries that.
- **Audience:** every existing account holder. They are precisely the people the
  grandfather promise covers, and the message is reassurance, not a sales pitch.

## Body (composer markup)

```
# A change to Imotara plans on 1 October

Hi {{name|there}},

From **1 October 2026**, Imotara will have two plans: a free plan, and Imotara Plus.

## If you already have an account, nothing changes

You keep everything you use today — permanently, at no cost.

We have already applied this to every existing account, including yours. There is nothing for you to do, no deadline to meet, and you will never be asked to pay to keep something you already had.

## What is changing, and for whom

This affects people who join Imotara **on or after 1 October**. For them, the free plan includes:

- Unlimited on-device conversations, which never leave the phone
- 20 cloud replies a day
- 7 days of conversation history
- Voice input, and replies read aloud

Imotara Plus adds unlimited cloud replies, your full history, search across it, data export, companion letters, your growth arc, and fine-grained control over the voice.

Imotara Plus is **₹149 a month**, or **₹1,299 a year** — about ₹108 a month.

## Why we are doing this at all

Imotara has been free for everyone while we built it. The conversations that need a larger model cost real money to run, and that cost grows with every person who finds the app useful. A paid plan is what lets Imotara keep running without ads, without selling anything about you, and without quietly making the free version worse.

That last part matters most to us, so we want to be plain about it: **the quality of your replies does not depend on what you pay.** A free reply and a paid reply are produced the same way. Plans change how much and how far back — never how well Imotara listens.

[[See what is in each plan]](https://www.imotara.com/upgrade)

---

Thank you for being here early. You are the reason there is something to charge for.

Soumen
Imotara
```
