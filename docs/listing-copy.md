# Imotara — the listing kit

One set of words for every directory, store and profile Imotara appears in.

**Why this file exists.** The ten audience PDFs drifted apart because the same facts were
re-typed in ten places — the Seniors sheet quietly lost a pricing tier, "22 languages" became
"all languages". Directory listings will drift the same way across eight sites unless the copy
lives somewhere once. Copy from here. If something here is wrong, fix it *here* and then fix the
listings.

**How this is written.** Like a person, not a marketing department. No "revolutionary",
no "seamless", no stacked feature bullets pretending to be a sentence. Someone looking for help
at 2am can smell a sales page, and they are the reader who matters.

Facts below were read out of the code on 2026-10-16, not remembered:
languages from `LANG_NAME` in `src/lib/connect/translate.ts`, pricing from `FREE_ROW` /
`PLUS_ROW` in `src/data/audiencePages.ts`.

## The one-liner (≤ 60 characters)

> Someone to talk to, in the language you think in.

Alternates, same register — use whichever fits the field:

- A private companion that listens in 22 languages.
- For the days you don't want to explain yourself.

## Short description (~140 characters)

> A private companion for the days you don't feel like explaining yourself. Speaks 22 languages,
> eleven of them Indian.

## Medium description (~250 characters)

> Imotara listens. It remembers what you told it last time. It speaks 22 languages — eleven
> Indian ones — and it works on a phone, a laptop, or offline on your own device. It is not
> therapy, and it doesn't pretend to be.

## Long description

> Most people don't want advice. They want to say the thing out loud to someone who won't flinch,
> change the subject, or tell the family.
>
> Imotara is that. You type or speak; it listens, asks one good question rather than three, and
> remembers what mattered last time you talked. It won't diagnose you, won't tell you to think
> positive, and won't panic if you say something frightening.
>
> It speaks 22 languages, and eleven of them are Indian — Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu,
> Gujarati, Punjabi, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia and Urdu — in ordinary conversational register, not
> textbook grammar. That matters more than it sounds. Most people feel things in their first
> language and only translate afterwards, and the translation is where the feeling gets lost.
>
> By default your conversations stay on your device. Cloud sync is something you switch on, not
> something you switch off. There are no ads, nothing is sold, and you can export or delete
> everything from one screen.
>
> If talking to software isn't enough, Imotara Connect puts you in touch with a verified human
> wellness companion, charged by the minute.
>
> **What it is not.** Imotara is not therapy, not a crisis service, and not a doctor. If you are
> in danger it will say so plainly and show you a real helpline for your country — but it is not
> a substitute for one.

carry a "who this is not for" list instead, and that discipline holds here.
