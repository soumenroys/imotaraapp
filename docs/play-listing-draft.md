# Play store listing — draft for review

**Status: DRAFT. Nothing submitted.** Changing a live listing is a public change, so this is
here for the owner to read first.

Play reviews store-listing text independently of app releases, so this can go live without a
new build — verified in the Console on 2026-10-16 (*Grow users → Store presence → Store
listings → Edit default listing* is enabled while the BillDesk release hold is in force).

Written to the house rule: like a person, not a marketing department. The current listing is
not bad, it is just *app-store voice* — emoji headers, `━━━━` dividers, feature bullets. For a
mental-health app that reads as a sales page, and the person who most needs Imotara is the one
most likely to bounce off one.

---

## 1. Title — 30 characters

**Now:** `Imotara` — 7 of 30 used.

This is the single biggest miss in the listing. On Play the title carries more ranking weight
than anything else, and 23 characters are doing nothing. Nobody searches "Imotara" who does not
already know it — which is exactly what Search Console shows for the website.

✅ **CHOSEN BY THE OWNER, 16 Oct 2026:** `Imotara: Mood & Mind Companion` *(30/30)*

> `Imotara: Mood & Mind Companion` *(30)*

⚠️ Keep `Imotara` first. The brand already ranks for its own name and that is the one thing
not worth losing.

## 3. Full description — 4000 characters

**Now:** 2205 characters. Roughly 1800 unused, and Play indexes this text.

What changes and why:

- **The dividers and most emoji go.** They date the listing and read as noise.
- **All eleven Indian languages are named.** Only four appear today. These are the words people
  actually search, and they are the genuine differentiator.
- **The opening stops selling and starts describing.** Three rhetorical questions in a row is
  the most sales-page thing in the current text.
- **The street address goes.** ⚠️ Play already publishes it in the mandatory "About the
  developer" block, so this removes the duplication, not the exposure.
- **The crisis note stays and gets plainer.** It is the most important paragraph in the listing.
