# YouTube — what to do with the channel

**Written 2026-10-16.** The channel is `youtube.com/@Imotara` — 22 subscribers, 20 videos,
all Shorts.

---

## The diagnosis

Recent videos have **0, 0, 1, 2, 5, 8 views.** That is not a reach problem, it is a
*what-is-this* problem. Look at the titles:

> "Talk. Connect. Feel heard. That's Imotara. 💛"
> "your Safe Place"
> "Nothing to worry Imotara is there 🤍"
> "#shorts"  ← this one is literally just the hashtag

**Nobody types any of those into a search box.** They're brand slogans — the words we want to
say, not the words anyone is looking for. One video's entire title is "#shorts".

### What the channel's own numbers already tell us

| Title | Views |
|---|---|
| "A happy heart makes a happy home 🏠" | **366** |
| "She deserves someone too 😔" | **131** |
| "Quick tutorial: How to chat with Imotara 💙" | **83** |
| "Talk. Breathe. Feel heard." | 64 |
| "This AI Doesn't Want Your Attention 😳" | 42 |
| …every product-slogan title | **0–9** |

🔑 **The two best performers by a wide margin are stories about a person. The worst are
pitches about the product.** The audience has already answered the question. We kept
publishing the pitch.

### And the differentiator is entirely absent

Twenty videos, **not one in Hindi, Bengali, Tamil or any of the other 21 languages** — the
single thing Imotara has that competitors don't. "Mental health app" is an impossible search
term. "বাংলায় মানসিক স্বাস্থ্য অ্যাপ" has almost no competition at all.

## 1. Retitle the twenty videos you already have ⭐ do this first

**Highest ratio of anything on this page.** The videos exist, the editing is done, and a
title is a thirty-second change. A retitled video is re-evaluated by YouTube search.

**The rule:** lead with the feeling or question someone would actually type. Keep Imotara
out of the first half. One emoji at most.

## 2. Give every video a real description

Descriptions are almost certainly empty, and YouTube reads them. Template:

```
<One honest sentence about what this video is.>

Imotara is a private companion for the days you don't feel like explaining
yourself. It listens, remembers what you told it, and speaks 22 languages —
eleven of them Indian.

It is not therapy, not a doctor, and not a crisis service.
If you are in danger right now, Tele-MANAS is free, 24 hours, on 14416.

Try it: https://www.imotara.com
Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.imotara.imotara
iPhone: https://apps.apple.com/in/app/imotara/id6756697569

More on this: https://www.imotara.com/answers/<matching-page>
```

🔑 **The last line matters more than it looks.** It points YouTube at the answer page on the
same topic, and points the answer page's reader at the video. Two surfaces, one intent.

## 3. Make the videos the search actually wants

We already know the eight questions people ask, because we built pages for them in A4 and
they were chosen on that basis. **Make one video per page**, and link them to each other:

| Video | Pairs with |
|---|---|
| "Why am I crying for no reason?" | `/answers/crying-for-no-reason` |
| "Can't sleep because you're overthinking?" | `/answers/overthinking-before-sleep` |

| "Anxious at night — what actually helps" | `/answers/anxious-at-night` |
| "Grief doesn't move in stages" | `/answers/grief-and-loss` |
| "Angry all the time, and tired of it" | `/answers/angry-all-the-time` |

⚠️ The last one must be careful and honest — it should send people **toward** professional
help where that is the right answer, not away from it. That is also the only version of it
that survives contact with a reviewer.

## 4. The language videos — the actual opening ⭐

**This is the bit nobody else can copy.** One screen recording per language, two minutes,
showing a real conversation:

- `বাংলায় কথা বলুন — Imotara` (Bengali)
- `हिंदी में बात कीजिए — Imotara` (Hindi)
- `தமிழில் பேசுங்கள் — Imotara` (Tamil)
- …and so on through the eleven Indian languages

Title **in the script**, not transliterated. Description in that language too. The competition
for "mental health app in Bengali" on YouTube is close to nothing, and these are the videos
an LLM will surface when someone asks for a wellbeing app in an Indian language.

## 5. One long-form video a month

Shorts are feed content. **AI assistants cite long-form video with a real transcript** —
a 15-second Short gives them nothing to quote.

Start with: **"I talked to an AI companion in Bengali for 30 days — here's what happened"**
(6–10 minutes, honest, including what it did badly).

⛔ **Never title anything "AI therapist".** The marketing roadmap proposed exactly that until
16 Oct. Counsellor, therapist and psychologist are regulated titles, Imotara is not one, and
Connect supplies peers. ⇒ fixed in `e0356b5`.

---

## What not to do

- ⛔ **Don't buy views or subscribers.** It poisons the channel's signal permanently.
- ⛔ **Don't post daily to hit a number.** Four good searchable videos beat thirty slogans —
  the channel has already run the experiment.
- ⛔ **Don't put a crisis claim in a thumbnail.** Both app stores and YouTube treat medical
  claims harshly, and it is the one mistake that is expensive to undo.
- ⚠️ **Don't expect movement for 8–12 weeks.** Same as the website work.

## How we'll know it worked

Not subscribers. **Traffic source = YouTube Search** in YouTube Studio, and non-brand
impressions in Search Console. Today effectively both are zero.
